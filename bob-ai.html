bob ai.

an assistant that compiles insurance assessment reports.

bob ai drafts reports for a south african insurance assessor, grounded in sans standards and the assessor’s own past reports, with summary and detailed modes and optional sans referencing.

it includes chat over an uploaded knowledge base, house-style templates, an independent accuracy check on every draft, and word and pdf export. built with next.js and supabase.
