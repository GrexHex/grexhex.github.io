iscarti.

upload your car’s service records and share a clear history with buyers.

iscarti keeps invoices, receipts and service records organised, with a public share link that shows the timeline only. no vin required.

every record carries a trust level assigned by the database, never by the client: owner-added, document checked, or workshop-confirmed through a one-time link. built with next.js, supabase and capacitor for android and ios.
